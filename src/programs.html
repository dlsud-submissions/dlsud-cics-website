Python PyTorch TensorFlow Hugging Face Scikit-learn Apache Spark

TRACK 02 // DEFENSE & INFRASTRUCTURE CICS / SPEC-SEC

Cybersecurity & Enterprise Networks

Master perimeter defense, penetration testing, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and Cisco enterprise switching. Prepare for Cisco CCNA, CompTIA Security+, and CEH industry credentials.

Key Technologies & Frameworks
Cisco IOS Kali Linux Wireshark Metasploit Snort IDS OpenSSL

TRACK 03 // CLOUD & ARCHITECTURE CICS / SPEC-CLOUD

Key Technologies & Frameworks
AWS Cloud Docker Kubernetes React.js Node / Express PostgreSQL
FACILITY: CICS Cloud Systems & Software Studio

Master of Science in Information Technology (MSIT)

The MSIT program is engineered for experienced technology leaders, senior software engineers, enterprise architects, and computing academicians. It balances executive strategic leadership with high-level applied research in emergent computational domains, artificial intelligence governance, and digital enterprise transformation.

01 Strategic Tech Leadership

IT enterprise governance, technology policy, and CIO digital transformation strategies.

02 Advanced Applied AI

Predictive modeling, automated decision pipelines, and ethical machine learning deployments.

03 Enterprise Cloud Defense

Zero-trust security postures, disaster resilience, and distributed microservices architecture.

04 Thesis & Publication

Mentored applied research culminating in peer-reviewed conference or journal contributions.

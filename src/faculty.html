02 — ADMINISTRATIVE STAFFBEHIND THE SCENES

Who keeps
things moving.

Administrative and technical staff support students and faculty every day. Contact the college to be routed to the right office.

OFFICE / 01

College Secretary

CICS Administrative Office

  • Manages the Dean's schedule and official correspondence.
  • Receives and routes student and faculty requests.
  • Maintains college records and announcements.
OFFICE / 02

Program Office Staff

CICS Program Office

  • Assists with advising schedules and enrollment queries.
  • Processes course and clearance documents.
  • Coordinates class schedules with program chairs.
OFFICE / 03

Laboratory Coordinator

CICS Laboratory Office

  • Oversees lab schedules, access, and equipment.
  • Supervises laboratory technicians and student assistants.
  • Enforces lab policies and safety procedures.
OFFICE / 04

OJT & Industry Coordinator

CICS Industry Partnerships

  • Maintains partner companies and placement options.
  • Reviews OJT documents and monitors student progress.
  • Collects supervisor feedback and final reports.
OFFICE / 05

Systems & Technical Support

CICS Technical Services

  • Maintains lab computers, network, and software licenses.
  • Responds to hardware and account issues.
  • Prepares equipment for classes and events.
OFFICE / 06

Student Affairs Liaison

CICS Student Affairs

  • Supports student organizations and college events.
  • Coordinates with the CICS Student Government.
  • Refers students to university support services.

* Staff roles describe the support functions that assist CICS faculty and students.

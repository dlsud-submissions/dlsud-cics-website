05 / LABORATORY SERVICES

Respect the lab.
Protect the work.

Laboratories are shared academic resources. Observe posted schedules and staff instructions at all times.

Computer lab rules +
  • Present a valid CICS or university identification when required.
  • Use assigned accounts and only approved software.
  • Keep stations clean, conserve resources, and report damage immediately.
  • Do not install, copy, or modify equipment without authorization.
Booking and access +
  • Reserve approved lab sessions through the responsible faculty or office.
  • Arrive on time and respect the end of the scheduled period.
  • Food, drinks, and unauthorized visitors are subject to lab restrictions.
Safety and conduct +
  • Follow equipment operating procedures and emergency instructions.
  • Keep walkways clear and do not tamper with network or power systems.
  • Report unsafe conditions to the lab supervisor or college office.
